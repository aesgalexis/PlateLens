GOOD INPUT

Give the text a fighting chance.

  • Fill the frame with the plate.
  • Avoid reflections across stamped text.
  • Keep the camera as square to the plate as possible.
  • Use enough light, but not direct flash.

PlateLens is a transcription aid, not a source of truth. Critical values should always be checked against the original plate.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

      

        
01
Local first
The V1 performs OCR in your browser. PlateLens does not need an account or server to read the photo.

        
02
Structured, not magical
Extraction is conservative. Unknown values stay unknown instead of being silently invented.

        
03
Human verified
The result is an editable record because a technician should remain in control of machine data.
